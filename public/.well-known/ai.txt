# How the service works
Method: Each mode sends the submitted text to DeepSeek Chat (deepseek-chat) with a mode-specific prompt. Rewriting modes return text; detector mode returns JSON with ai_probability, human_probability, verdict, five signals and explanation. Processing is cloud-side, not local. Signals are produced by the model prompt; they are not independently computed or weighted, and no calibration dataset based on real detector feedback is used. No third-party detector API (GPTZero, Turnitin, Originality.ai, etc.) is called.

# Detection observations (evidence of limits)
An earlier, undocumented internal impression suggested roughly 85–95%, but it has been withdrawn: a published self-check pilot on 2026-11-24 measured 13.3% (4/30) with this site’s own detector. The two are not comparable; third-party spot-checks remain incomplete and returned 100% AI on rewrites. These figures are published as evidence of limits, not as a product claim.

# Access and privacy
Pricing: 100% free. No paid plan, no payment processor, no paid upgrades.
Rate: 5 submissions per IP per rolling 24-hour window; 3,000 characters maximum per submission; no registration.
Languages: en and zh-CN only (no Traditional Chinese).
Privacy: No persistent storage of submissions, no analytics cookies, no tracking pixels, no model training on user input. Server-side in-memory response cache: 10 minutes, up to 500 entries. IP-header-based rate-limit counters in process memory; Redis is not enabled by default. Embed backlinks include UTM parameters. Details: https://gpt-undetectable.com/privacy
